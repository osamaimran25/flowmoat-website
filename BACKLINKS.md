## Long description (~120 words)

Flowmoat is an AI engineering and automation company working with businesses in the United States, the United Kingdom and Europe. We build RAG assistants that answer from a company's own documents with citations, AI agents and copilots, document processing pipelines, and workflow automation with n8n and Python, inside the tools teams already use.

Clients hire us for a fixed-scope first project, or add a dedicated AI engineer to their team on a monthly basis. Systems are designed around GDPR and UK GDPR, can run in EU cloud regions, and always keep a person approving consequential decisions.

We also build and operate our own products: RegRely, an AI compliance and privacy platform, and GreenOH, a solar marketplace.

## Don't

Buy links, use PBNs or link farms, or post fake reviews. Google discounts or penalises them, and fake reviews are illegal in the US (FTC rule) and UK (DMCC Act).
